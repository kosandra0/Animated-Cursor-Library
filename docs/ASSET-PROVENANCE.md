# Asset provenance and licensing

## Supplied archives

The library was assembled from these user-provided inputs:
- `Glow-Cursor-Forge-Auto-Theme-Installer(1).zip`
- `GlowCursors (main).rar`
- `NeonPulse_Cursors_With_AutoInstaller.rar`

The 16-theme ZIP was copied byte-for-byte into the local bundle. The two original RAR files were retained byte-for-byte under `source-archives/`. The RAR collections were extracted and repacked as convenient ZIP downloads. The extracted assets retain their pack-specific folder structure and installers.
